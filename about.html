Manifesto

We believe growing up does not mean becoming boring.

Monsterpp makes clothes for people who still laugh too loudly, feel too much, and refuse to act normal. Playful but wearable. Cute but not innocent. Weird but well-designed.

The story

It started with a doodle.

A black furry blob with two big eyes, drawn in the margin of a very boring notebook. It refused to leave. So we gave it horns, a few friends, and a wardrobe.

Today Monsterpp is a small studio obsessed with one question: how do we make everyday things more fun — without making them childish? Every drop is designed in-house, sampled obsessively, and approved by at least one monster.

Designed in-house

Every graphic, character and garment starts on paper in our studio.

Made to be worn

Heavyweight fabrics, real fits, gender-free sizing. Weird, not flimsy.

Better when we can

Responsible materials and small, considered runs over landfill fashion.

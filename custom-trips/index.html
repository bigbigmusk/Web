How it works

Western China, thoughtfully planned

There are no fixed packages here. Every itinerary is written from a blank page and built around the people travelling — your dates, your interests, the pace you enjoy and the level of comfort you want. Whether you have a clear route in mind or only a rough idea of where to begin, we'll shape a private journey that fits.

Behind the planning is a local team on the ground in Western China, working with guides and drivers who know their home regions well. We communicate in clear English throughout, and we give honest advice on what is realistic given the seasons, the altitude and the long distances out west — so the trip you travel is the trip you were promised.

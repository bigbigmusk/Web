How booking works

Reliable from request to trip card

Many local experiences depend on real guides and vehicles, so we confirm with the supplier before you go — and keep you informed at every step.

  1. 1

    Choose & pay

    Pick your date, group size and options. See the full price, what's included and the cancellation policy before paying.

  2. 2

    We confirm

    Your order goes to paid · pending confirmation. We confirm the guide and resources within the booking SLA — usually under 4 hours.

  3. 3

    Get your trip card

    A bilingual trip card with meeting point, English + Chinese address, your guide and an emergency contact — easy to screenshot.

  4. 4

    Enjoy & review

    Meet your local guide and go. After your trip, share feedback and refer a friend.

⚖️ If a supplier can't confirm, we'll offer an alternative or a full refund — no order is ever left waiting without a human following up.

Help center

Questions, answered before you book

How do I pay, and is it secure?

Pay online through a trusted payment provider. You'll get an order number and a record of everything you booked.

What does "request to confirm" mean?

Some experiences need a real guide or vehicle, so we confirm with the supplier after you pay — usually within 4 hours. If we can't, you get an alternative or a full refund.

What's your cancellation policy?

Each product shows its own policy before you pay, including the free-cancellation window. No surprises at checkout.

Where will I meet my guide?

Your trip card includes the meeting point on a map, the address in English and Chinese, your guide's details and an emergency contact.

I don't speak Chinese — is that ok?

Yes. Our experiences are built for English-speaking visitors, with English support before and during your trip.

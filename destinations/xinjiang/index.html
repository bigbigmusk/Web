Overview

Xinjiang at a Glance

Xinjiang is China's largest region and one of the most rewarding stretches of the historic Silk Road. In the south-west, Kashgar remains its cultural centre: the Old Town's lanes of mud-brick houses, the famous Sunday bazaar and a daily rhythm shaped by Uyghur life, language and food. From here the Karakoram road climbs into the Pamir toward the high, mirror-still waters of Karakul Lake beneath snow peaks.

To the east, the oasis city of Turpan sits in one of the lowest, warmest basins in the country, known for its grapes, the ancient ruined city of Jiaohe and the karez underground irrigation channels that have watered the desert for centuries. The regional capital, Urumqi, is the usual gateway and a good base for the Tianshan mountains, while the vast Taklamakan Desert defines the empty heart of the region.

Food is a highlight of any visit — hand-pulled noodles, freshly baked naan, and lamb grilled at lively night markets. Distances are large and routes can change with conditions, so we plan Xinjiang itineraries with realistic pacing, the right internal flights and guides who know the area well.

Best time to visit

May to October. Late spring and autumn are comfortable across most of the region; summer is hot in the desert basins but ideal for the high Pamir and mountain lakes.

Best for

Silk Road history, desert and mountain landscapes, lively bazaars and markets, Uyghur culture and food, and some of the best photography in Western China.

Getting around

Distances are long, so itineraries combine internal flights with private drives. Routes and checkpoints can change, so we build in flexibility and keep plans current.

Itineraries

Suggested Route Ideas

Starting points we can shape around your dates, pace and interests. Each is private, with your own guide and vehicle.

Kashgar & the Pamir — 6–8 days

Time in Kashgar's Old Town and Sunday bazaar, then the Karakoram road south into the Pamir to Karakul Lake beneath Muztagh Ata. A focused route for travelers drawn to mountains, markets and Uyghur culture.

Silk Road Loop: Urumqi, Turpan & Kashgar — 10–12 days

A broader Silk Road journey linking Urumqi and the Tianshan, the vineyards and ancient ruins of Turpan, and Kashgar with its bazaar and the Pamir. Combines internal flights with scenic drives across the region.

Frequently Asked Questions

Most international travelers need a tourist (L) visa for mainland China. We provide a confirmed itinerary and booking documents to support your application, and advise on the latest rules and any visa-free transit options for your nationality.

Yes. Foreign travelers visiting Tibet need a Tibet Travel Permit and must travel with a registered guide on a pre-arranged itinerary. We arrange the permit and guide for you as part of any Tibet journey.

Western China is generally very safe, with low petty crime. The main considerations are altitude, long distances and weather, which we plan around carefully — with local support on call throughout your trip.

We accept international bank transfer and major cards for trip payments. On the ground we help you set up mobile payments such as WeChat Pay and Alipay, which are used almost everywhere in China, and advise on carrying some cash for remote areas.

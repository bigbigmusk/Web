03 — Craft

Built from absence.

Every VANTAIRE shoe is defined as much by what is removed as by what remains. We cut, weave, and lattice full-grain leather into structures that hold their shape while letting light and air pass through.

Lattice leather
Full-grain hides cut into open lattice panels — architectural, never flimsy.
Breathable structure
Cut-outs engineered for airflow, designed for heat and long days on foot.
Hand-woven feeling
Each band is hand-set, giving the woven warmth of a craft object.
Cushioned footbed
A vegetable-tanned, padded leather footbed that softens with wear.
Flexible outsole
A whisper-thin, hand-stitched sole that bends with every step.

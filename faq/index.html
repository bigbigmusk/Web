Home/FAQ

Most nationalities need a visa to visit China, usually a tourist (L) visa applied for before you travel. We provide a confirmed itinerary and booking confirmation to support your application. Some nationalities qualify for visa-free transit or short visa-free entry under specific conditions, so always confirm the current rules with your nearest Chinese embassy or consulate before booking flights.

Western China is generally safe for international travelers, with low levels of petty crime. The main things to plan for are altitude, long distances and weather rather than safety concerns. We design sensible routes, build in time to acclimatise where needed, and provide local support throughout. You can read more in our guide on whether Western China is safe.

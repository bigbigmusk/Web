Highlights

Trip highlights

  • Kashgar Old Town and the famous Sunday livestock bazaar, the trading heart of the southern Silk Road.
  • Karakul Lake beneath Muztagh Ata in the Pamirs, framed by snow peaks and high pasture.
  • The Karakoram Highway, one of the great mountain roads, climbing from oasis to plateau.
  • Turpan's Jiaohe ruins, the ancient karez irrigation channels and the green grape valleys.
  • The edge of the Taklamakan Desert, where the Silk Road skirted vast dunes.
  • Uyghur food at its source — slow-cooked lamb, fresh-baked naan and hand-pulled laghman.
  • Heavenly Lake of Tianshan near Urumqi, an alpine lake ringed by spruce forest and peaks.
Day by day

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1

Arrive Urumqi

Land in Urumqi, the regional capital and your gateway to Xinjiang. Your guide meets you at the airport and transfers you to your hotel. The rest of the day is free to rest after your flight and settle into the time zone.

Day 2

Urumqi to Kashgar

A short domestic flight carries you south-west to Kashgar, the oldest oasis city on the Silk Road. After arrival we take an easy first walk through the old quarter to get our bearings, then enjoy a welcome Uyghur meal.

Day 3

Kashgar Old Town and craftsmen's streets

A full day exploring Kashgar on foot: the lanes of the Old Town, the Id Kah area at the city's center, and the workshops where coppersmiths, woodcarvers and instrument makers still work by hand.

Day 4

Kashgar Sunday bazaar and livestock market

If your trip includes a Sunday, we visit the weekly grand bazaar and the lively livestock market on the city's edge, where traders gather to buy and sell sheep, cattle and horses — one of the most atmospheric scenes in Central Asia.

Day 5

Kashgar to Karakul Lake via the Karakoram Highway

We drive up the Karakoram Highway into the Pamir mountains, with stops for photography as the landscape shifts from oasis to high plateau. We reach Karakul Lake (around 3,600m) beneath Muztagh Ata and stay nearby.

Day 6

Karakul Lake and the Pamir

A relaxed morning by the lake, with time to walk along the shore, meet local Kyrgyz herders and take in the peaks reflected in the water. In the afternoon we return down the highway to Kashgar.

Day 7

Kashgar to Turpan

We fly from Kashgar towards Turpan, usually connecting via Urumqi. Turpan sits in a hot desert basin below sea level and has been a Silk Road crossroads for centuries. The evening is at leisure among its shaded vine trellises.

Day 8

Turpan: Jiaohe, karez and grape valley

We visit the cliff-set ruins of Jiaohe, learn how the ancient karez underground channels brought meltwater across the desert, see the Flaming Mountains and walk through the green grape valley that the oasis is known for.

Day 9

Turpan to Urumqi and Heavenly Lake

We travel back to Urumqi and continue to the Heavenly Lake of Tianshan, an alpine lake set among spruce forest and snow peaks. Time permitting, we also visit the Xinjiang regional museum for context on the Silk Road.

Day 10

Departure

A relaxed morning before your transfer to Urumqi airport for your onward flight. Your guide sees you off, and we remain on call should you need anything as you travel on.

Seasons

Best time to travel

This route works best from May to October. In high summer the Turpan basin is genuinely hot, often well above 35°C, while the Pamirs around Karakul Lake stay cool and fresh — so the itinerary moves between very different climates in a single trip. Pack layers for both.

The shoulder months of May, June, September and October are particularly rewarding: daytime temperatures are more comfortable, the light is clear for photography, and the autumn grape and fruit harvest around Turpan adds to the experience. Outside this window mountain access can be limited by snow, so we plan accordingly.

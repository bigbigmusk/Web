Our story

Why We Exist

Most first trips to China follow the same well-worn route: Beijing, Shanghai, perhaps Xi'an. It is a fine introduction, but it leaves out the part of the country that many travelers find the most rewarding — the high plateaus, Silk Road cities, sacred mountains and the ethnic cultures of the West. We started China West Journeys to help international travelers reach that deeper, more cinematic and culturally rich side of China, with a team that genuinely knows the ground.

We focus on one region and learn it properly. China West Journeys is a boutique inbound travel company specializing in private Western China tours for international travelers. Rather than offer a global catalogue, we concentrate on Tibet, Xinjiang, Sichuan and Chengdu, Yunnan, Qinghai and the Gansu Silk Road — six regions we travel, study and plan in continuously.

Every trip is private and planned by a person, not assembled from a fixed package. We listen first, then draft a route around your dates, pace and interests, and adjust it until it feels right. We care about cultural respect too: traveling in the West means moving through living Tibetan, Uyghur, Naxi, Bai and other communities, and we plan in a way that treats places and people with care.

When you travel, you are supported on the ground by local guides and drivers, with clear English communication before, during and after your trip. The aim is simple — to make a part of China that can feel hard to reach feel approachable, well-organised and worth the journey.

What we value

How We Like to Travel

A handful of principles shape every itinerary we write and every trip we run.

Local knowledge

We plan with people who live in the regions you visit — so advice is current, practical and grounded in real conditions on the road.

Western China expertise

We focus on one part of the world and study it in depth, from Tibet's plateau to the Silk Road, rather than spreading across a global list.

Human planning

Your trip is designed by a person you can talk to — no call-centre scripts and no fixed packages, just a clear conversation about your trip.

Cultural respect

The West is home to living Tibetan, Uyghur and many other communities. We plan in a way that treats local people, faiths and places with care.

Reliable travel support

Private guides, drivers and a team on call mean help is close at hand. Transfers, trains and flights are coordinated end to end.

Honest advice

If a route is too rushed, a season is wrong or an idea will not work well, we say so. We would rather plan a trip that genuinely suits you.

How we work

What to Expect

Working with us starts with a conversation, not a checkout. You share what you have in mind, we draft a private itinerary, and we refine it together until the route, hotels and pace feel right. From there our local team handles the logistics so you can focus on the journey.

Many travelers begin from one of our sample tours and adapt it, while others prefer to start from a blank page with a custom trip. Either way, every itinerary ends up built around you.

Global expansion fails for one reason.

Most companies do not fail at global expansion because their products are bad. They fail because they do not know which market to enter, who to sell to, how competitors position similar products, and what sales assets to use.

Which market?

Teams guess at geography instead of scoring real demand, competition, and price potential.

Who to sell to?

No clear ideal customer profile, so outreach is scattered and conversion stays low.

Competitor blind spots

You never see how rivals position, price, and message the same product abroad.

No sales assets

Great products arrive with no English collateral, decks, or proof to close buyers.
